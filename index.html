MY MANIFESTO

What I’ll do for SCI.

Collaborations

Connect clubs and project teams

I’ll bring SCI clubs, IEEE, AWS student groups and students from other programmes together for joint projects. A team-matching session will help newcomers find a role, even before they have a portfolio.

Sessions

Give students a place to ask

I’ll invite seniors, alumni and practitioners to small Q&A sessions. Students can suggest topics and send questions ahead of time, from choosing electives to preparing for an internship.

Workshops

Build something you can show

I’ll organise hands-on workshops where you can build a first website, analyse a dataset or prototype an AI tool. I’ll ask facilitators to share the starter files so anyone can practise afterwards.

New opportunities

Put deadlines within reach

I’ll bring internships, hackathons, scholarships and speaker calls into one student-accessible calendar. Each listing should say who can apply, when it closes and where to start.

A stronger SCI

Give student ideas a follow-up

I’ll take recurring student concerns to the committee and share updates on what was raised, what was agreed and what still needs work. You can send me an idea directly below.
